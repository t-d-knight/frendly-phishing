Don't worry, nothing happened

This page is run by the Cyber Security team for Cyber Security Awareness Month. We didn't collect your name, your device, your location, or anything else. We only add one to the counter above.

So what could a real one have done?

  • Shown a fake staff login page to steal your password
  • Asked you to "confirm your details" for the prize
  • Pushed an app or file to your phone
  • Set off an MFA prompt it wanted you to approve

QR codes are just links you can't read. A sticker slapped over a real one looks exactly the same.

Before you scan, ask

  • Who put this here? Official posters come from somewhere you can check.
  • Is it too good to be true? Free stuff plus urgency is the oldest trick there is.
  • Check the link preview your camera shows before you tap it.
  • Never enter work passwords on a page you reached from a QR code.
  • Not sure? Report it. You won't get in trouble, and we would much rather hear about it.
